Entry for the make.fun bounty "The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue" — a ~100-word monologue from Dread Lich Malachar, scourge of seven kingdoms, who loses his grimoire mid-boast, pauses for tea, and ends up ordering the hero to sit instead of kneel.

> So. You have climbed the Withered Spire, past my skeletons — good lads, mostly, though Brunt keeps losing his left arm in the stairwell — and here I stand, the Dread Lich Malachar, scourge of seven kingdoms, keeper of the Grimoire, and — hold on — where did I put the grimoire? It was here. Anyway. The plan is simple: at midnight I raise every grave in the valley, and they will march on the capital — ah, but first, tea. My throat. The dead can wait; they are very patient, the dead. Now, kneel, or... actually, sit. Both of us. Sit.
